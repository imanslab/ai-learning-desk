# Contributing

> [Read this in Persian](CONTRIBUTING.fa.md). That translation is informational.

Corrections and new cards are welcome. Read CLA.md first. A pull request needs
one line saying you agree to it.

## What a good card has

- A question on the front that a learner would ask.
- The idea, the technique, one example and one pitfall, each in one or two
  short sentences.
- A link to the original paper or the official documentation, when one exists.
- Your own words. Summarise the source. Do not copy it.

## Rules for every change

**Check the source before you change a card.** A confident wrong sentence is
worse than no sentence, because a reader concludes there is nothing to check.
Say in the pull request what you checked and where.

**One idea per card.** If a card needs two ideas, make two cards.

**Examples are illustrations.** An example must not make a claim about a real
company, market or person that the linked source does not support.

**Plain English.** Short sentences. Explain a term the first time it appears,
then use the same term every time.

## House rules on output

No emoji. No ANSI escape codes. No em dashes. Anywhere, including commit
messages, branch names and tags. Use plain hyphens.

## Commit messages

Say what changed and why. The only author of a commit is the person who made
it. Do not add tool, bot or assistant names as authors or co-authors.

## Before you open a pull request

Open index.html in a browser. Turn the card you changed, search for it, and
check that the page shows no errors in the browser console.
