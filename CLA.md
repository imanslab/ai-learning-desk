## What you confirm

That the work is yours to give: you wrote it, or you have the right to submit
it, and no agreement with an employer or anyone else contradicts this.

That a card you add is in your own words. A card may summarise a paper or a
documentation page and link to it. It may not copy that source's text.

That any third-party material in your contribution is named, with its licence.

## Why this exists

The cards are free for non-commercial use, and the author reserves commercial
rights so that paid editions, courses and commercial licences stay possible.
Without this agreement, every contributor holds copyright in their own edit,
and one contributor who disagrees, or cannot be reached, could block a
commercial licence for the whole collection.

## What this does NOT do

It does not take your copyright. It does not stop the cards being free for
non-commercial use. It does not oblige the author to accept your contribution.

## How to accept

Say so in your first pull request:

    I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.

## A limit worth stating plainly

not drafted by a lawyer. If this agreement ever matters commercially, replace
it with a reviewed one.
