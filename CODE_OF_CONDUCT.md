[فارسی](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fa.md) | **English**

> [Read this in Persian](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fa.md). That translation is informational.

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the card, not the person.

## What is expected

Disagreement about a card is welcome. Take it to the evidence: link the paper,
quote the documentation, show the output. "This card is wrong because the paper
says X on page 4" is a contribution. "This card is wrong because you do not
know the subject" is not.

Assume the person you are replying to is competent and has a reason.

Say when you are unsure. An honest "I have not checked this" is worth more
here than confidence.

## What is not acceptable

Personal attacks, harassment, and dismissing somebody because of who they are
rather than what they wrote. Publishing anybody's private information.
Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Learners are welcome

Many readers are new to the subject. A basic question is a good question. Do
not mock it, and do not answer it with a link and nothing else.

## Reporting

Through the contact on the author's GitHub profile, https://github.com/Iman.
Reports are read by the maintainer and are not made public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close pull requests, and block accounts.
There is no process more elaborate than that, and pretending otherwise would be
dishonest for a project this size.
